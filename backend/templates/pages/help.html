Frequently Asked Questions

How does Finora calculate my credit score?

Finora combines traditional bureau metrics (payment history, credit utilization, length of history) with dynamic AI behavior analysis to provide a real-time, 100% explainable credit model.

Will using Finora hurt my official credit score?

No! Finora uses soft credit inquiries only. Checking your score or receiving AI recommendations on Finora never impacts your official credit rating.

Is my bank and financial data safe?

Yes. Finora uses SOC2 Type II certified infrastructure with 256-bit AES encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. We never sell your personal data.
