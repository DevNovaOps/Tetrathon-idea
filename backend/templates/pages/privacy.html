Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026. Complete transparency on how your data is collected and used.

1. Information We Collect

Finora collects minimal necessary information required to compute credit intelligence and personalized wealth recommendations:

  • Account registration data (Name, Email, Encrypted credentials).
  • Self-reported or connected financial telemetry (utilization ratios, transaction categories).
  • Technical metrics (device type, IP address, operating system, anonymous cookies).

2. How We Use Your Data

We process your data strictly to execute real-time AI scoring, generate tax statements, simulate scenario outcomes, and improve model accuracy. We NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties or advertisers.

3. Your Rights Under GDPR & CCPA

You have full ownership of your data at all times:

  • Right to Access: Export a full CSV/JSON copy of your data at any time from Settings.
  • Right to Erasure: Delete your account and purge all associated database records instantly.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Toggle off optional analytical telemetry in your preferences.

4. Contact Us

For questions regarding privacy practices or data subject requests, contact privacy@finora.ai.
